O Cronista Terminal is a personal blog and, even though I intend to write quite a few tutorials, I have no wish to build a community around it: knowledge is free, whether for one or for all. I should also stress that **I have no commercial plans for this blog** and, if I did, my attitude toward using AI would be very different.

Even so, it matters for anyone who produces any kind of content, and especially for people in tech, to be transparent about how they use AI in their content and to take a position on the subject. This page is exactly that.

I believe the unchecked and irresponsible use of artificial intelligence may cause more harm in the medium and long term than we are able to understand today. As many grandparents used to say: *O seguro morreu de velho* — roughly, "better safe than sorry". That said, I believe the *ai-assisted* mindset is much healthier than *ai-first*. Below is a list of where AI is used today in the development and production of this blog:

**Development:**

- Brainstorming and researching styles.
- Support in developing the template.
- Usability testing.
- Simplifying and optimizing code.
- Documentation and versioning in git.
- Building tools to use, and to generate assets through code.

It is quite easy, and I made a point of keeping it that way, to tell when something in the code was done by AI: the habit of adding comments to the code makes it clear to the reader where it had more autonomy and where I did more of the work.

**Writing:**

- Gathering references for the post.
- Help with gathering images.
- Producing diagrams.
- Correcting grammar, coherence and cohesion.
- Adding and maintaining the bibliography.
- Generating the short and long tl;dr.
- Generating the post's tags.
- Fitting the content into the templates of Hugo (the engine I use for the blog).

**Translation:**

The English translation, when present, will be done in two stages: an automatic translation by artificial intelligence, and then a human translation, done by me. Given how important English is to the development community, I felt torn about not having an English version available sooner. For now, that is the tradeoff I have accepted.
